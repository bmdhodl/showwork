# Research sources: forked-export provenance copy contract

Date: 2026-08-15

## Current wording

The evidence-pack header says it was generated by `evidence_pack.py` from the
ledger at `.showwork/`. The pack disclaimer calls it supporting evidence
prepared by the operator and not a certification. The audit JSON, but not the
rendered pack, carries `forks`, `heads`, `break_at`, and `detail`. The pack does
not state source checkout, commit, agent, or missing provenance.

## Candidate labels

1. `Owner-local evidence — fork-aware chain; not an independent endorsement.`
2. `Provenance unavailable — this export does not include source checkout,
   commit, or agent identity.`

## Case interpretation

| Case | Label decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owner-operated linear export | candidate 1, with actual owner context | makes local scope explicit; does not claim authorship or adoption |
| owner-operated forked export | candidates 1 + 2, plus audit JSON | fork heads are real audit data; the pack alone omits them |
| cloned or mirrored export | candidate 2 | a copy is not an independent endorsement |
| unknown public artifact | candidate 2 | missing attribution must remain visible; do not infer adoption |
| signed or hardware-attested artifact | out of scope | wording must not imply either property exists in showwork |

## Recommendation

NO CHANGE to packer or public copy. Keep both labels in this research artifact
only. If an owner-gated public wording request arrives, use the narrowest label
that matches the evidence and keep “not an independent endorsement” adjacent
to any fork/export reference. Do not add a provenance format, signing, second
verifier, or adoption language from this comparison.
